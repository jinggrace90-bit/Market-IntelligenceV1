The Core Data: What is Freemasonry?

Freemasonry is a fraternal organization focused on self-improvement. It is not a religion or a political group.

The Three Principles

  • Brotherly Love: Tolerance and respect among members.
  • Relief: Charity and community service.
  • Truth: High moral standards and honesty.

The Four Virtues

  • Temperance: Balance in life.
  • Fortitude: Courage in adversity.
  • Prudence: Thoughtful decision-making.
  • Justice: Fairness and equality.

The Core Symbols

Square

Morality and honesty.

Compasses

Self-discipline and appropriate boundaries.

Letter 'G'

Geometry (order of the universe) and God (the Supreme Being).

The Core Goal

"Making good men better."

Freemasonry seeks to take upright citizens and give them tools to better serve family and community.
